# Model and creator icons

The files in `icons/` come from
[architect-xyz/inference-catalog](https://github.com/architect-xyz/inference-catalog)
(commit 8deecc0490e9ecf4d4b18c2904d7cd498b482df3), whose `logos/icons/` folder
is the artwork of [TypingMind's model-icons](https://github.com/TypingMind/model-icons),
under the MIT license in `LICENSE.txt`.

The license covers the collection. The logos are the marks of their owners
and do not imply endorsement. `scripts/vendor-inference-catalog.mjs` refreshes
the files at a pinned commit.
